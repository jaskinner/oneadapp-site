== main
In closed beta with Cleveland producers. Capped at 3 to 5 producers. Book your crew. Rehire the best. Invite crew by email. They accept or decline from a link, no account needed. Everyone you hire goes into a private rehire list only you can see. Crew are free. Works alongside the tools you already use, like StudioBinder, Movie Magic and Wrapbook. Made for indie producers, line producers, UPMs, and ADs who book crew by text and email today. Request beta access Confirmations get lost between texts and sheets You are already booking crew by text and email, then checking a sheet to see who said yes. oneAD keeps that answer on the project: who you invited, and who accepted. How it works Create a project. Add departments, then the roles in each department. Add the shoot days. Invite crew by email. They accept or decline from the link. No account is required. The link expires in 14 days. Each department lists its roles, who is booked, and which invitations are still out. A day, or the whole production When you invite someone, choose the shoot days they are booked. Leave the days unchecked and they are booked for the whole production. After they are booked, you can change those days. Relay: one email to the crew who have accepted. Send it to everyone, one department, or one shoot day. Pending invitations are not included. Relays go out from the project owner. A private crew book When someone accepts, they are saved to your private crew book. You can rate them from 1 to 5 and keep notes. Ratings and notes stay private to you. The next time you invite for a role, people you have already hired for that role are listed first, highest rating first. You can still type a new email. Crew are free. Pricing is planned. Accepting an invite does not require a payment. Crew are free. Pricing for producers is planned. No prices are shown here, and the app does not take payment today. Request beta access In closed beta with Cleveland producers. Capped at 3 to 5 producers. oneAD is invite-only right now. Request access and Jonathan will follow up.
