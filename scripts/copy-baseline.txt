== main
Closed beta · Cleveland producers Book your crew. Rehire the best. Invite film crew by email. They accept or decline from a link, no account needed. Everyone you hire goes into a private crew book only you can see. Made for indie film producers, line producers, UPMs, and ADs who book crew by text and email today. Request beta access Use it next to tools like StudioBinder, Movie Magic and Wrapbook. oneAD doesn't connect to them. Confirmations get lost between texts and sheets You are already booking crew by text and email, then checking a sheet to see who said yes. oneAD keeps that answer on the project: who you invited, and who accepted. How film crew booking works Create a project. Add departments, then the roles in each department. Add the shoot days. Invite film crew by email. They accept or decline from the link, no account needed. The link expires in 14 days. Each department lists its roles, who is booked, and which invitations are still out. Book crew by the day or for the whole production When you invite someone, choose the shoot days they are booked. Leave the days unchecked and they are booked for the whole production. After they are booked, you can change those days. A relay is one email to the crew who have accepted. Send it to everyone, one department, or one shoot day. Pending invitations are not included. Only the project owner can send relays. A private crew book When someone accepts, they are saved to your private crew book. You can rate them from 1 to 5 and keep notes. Ratings and notes stay private to you. The next time you invite for a role, people you have already hired for that role are listed first, highest rating first. You can still type a new email. Crew are free. Pricing is planned. Accepting an invite does not require a payment. Crew are free. Pricing for producers is planned, and the app does not take payment today. Join the closed beta In closed beta with Cleveland producers. Capped at 3 to 5 producers. Beta spots are limited. Send your name, email and the kind of production you work on, and Jonathan will reply about next steps. Request beta access
