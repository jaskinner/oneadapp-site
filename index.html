Confirmations get lost between texts and sheets

You are already booking crew by text and email, then checking a sheet to see who said yes. oneAD keeps that answer on the project: who you invited, and who accepted.

How it works

  1. Create a project.
  2. Add departments, then the roles in each department.
  3. Add the shoot days.
  4. Invite crew by email.
  5. They accept or decline from the link. No account is required. The link expires in 14 days.

On the project, each department lists the roles, who is booked, and which invitations are still out. A pending invite is marked “Invitation Sent”. Someone who has accepted is marked accepted.

A day, or the whole production

When you invite someone, choose the shoot days they are booked. Leave the days unchecked and they are booked for the whole production. After they are booked, you can change those days.

A relay is an email you send to crew who have accepted. Pending invitations are not included. Send it to the whole crew, to one department, or only to the people booked on one shoot day. Only the project owner can send a relay.

A private crew book

When someone accepts, they are saved to Contacts on the project owner’s account. Only that owner can open the book. You can rate someone from 1 to 5 and keep notes. Ratings and notes stay private to you.

The next time you invite for a role, people you have already hired for that role are listed first, highest rating first. You can still type a new email.

If a crew member has a verified account, pending invitations for their email are listed under Invitations, where they can accept or decline. The email link works without an account.
